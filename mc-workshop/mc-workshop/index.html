How to prepare

One thing. That's it. Come ready with your "Steal With Pride" slide — one big idea from your market that could move the needle for someone else in the room.

Your one task before the workshop
"Steal With Pride" Session
One slide. One big idea. 20 minutes.
If there is one thing you've done in acquisition that genuinely worked, what is it?
💡
THE
IDEA
What did you do?
Present the cool thing that worked — one tactic, one initiative, one channel moment.
🚀
WHY IT
WORKED
Key result or impact.
Keep it concrete — a number, a rate, a behaviour change you observed.
⏱️
YOUR
TIME
20 min total.
15' present + 5' Q&A
One slide only — no exceptions.
📬 Send your slide before 27 May 2026 to let the team prepare material before the workshop.
1 SLIDE ONLY SHORT & PUNCHY NO 20-SLIDE DECKS NO MARKET OVERVIEWS
🎯
Focus on the idea that transfers — not the market context. Others need to steal it.
🖼️
Make it visual and memorable — one slide that people photograph during the session.
📤
Send the slide to Carine before 27 May so the team can align.
🤝
Come ready to ask questions of the other markets — the Q&A is where the real value happens.
