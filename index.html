Market Diagnostic Insights

Key themes and priorities surfaced across 8 pre-workshop interviews — one per market.

Between April and May 2026, each participating market took part in a structured diagnostic conversation. The sessions explored acquisition ambition, channel performance, consumer journey strengths, and priorities for the Lausanne workshop. What follows is a forward-looking synthesis — not a debrief, but a springboard for what we'll build together.

What's working well
Growth opportunity
Workshop ask
🇪🇬Egypt
Region 2 — MENA
Building a seamless, multi-category lead generation engine
Strong CC and P1 base — among the highest SFP acquisition markets in the region
MC Events activation already underway — El Gouna model shows consumer continuation potential
Scaling AI-powered segmentation to reduce manual profiling and improve journey routing
Unlocking P4 alongside P1 to drive more leads into the ecosystem simultaneously
Workshop priority
"We need a framework for knowing when to introduce the second category — and a smarter way to route consumers from day one."
🇲🇦Morocco
Region 2 — MENA
Repositioning P5/ZYN and preparing for the Bonds & Blends launch
High ZYN brand awareness (~75%) — strong recognition to build on
LAMP engagement performing well, notably post-AFCON activation momentum
Reframing ZYN as modern and aspirational — moving beyond "kala" perception
Enriching consumer data ahead of the Bonds & Blends July launch
Workshop priority
"We want to understand how P1 and P5 can work as distinct, complementary roles — and how to build post-engagement journeys for non-registered ZYN users."
🇪🇸Spain
Region 3 — Europe
Data-driven CX model with strong central governance
All MC content centrally filtered by CX before reaching frontline — minimising overload
Strong analytics integration; guided trial with 3 flavours shows significantly higher conversion
Scaling the new B2C consumer journey rolling out from June across all geographies
Closing the gap between brand-led campaigns and actual POS shopper profile
Workshop priority
"We're looking for peer-market input on POS navigation, icebreakers, and portfolio storytelling — practical execution models, not frameworks."
🇦🇹Austria
Region 3 — Europe
Navigating a highly restricted environment with precision
Strong retail partnerships — clear trade engagement model in place
Experiential channels (LAMP/events) becoming a meaningful trial driver
Solving traffic routing — retailers resist redirecting consumers even with incentives
Building scalable attribution for ~800 stores under tight system constraints
Workshop priority
"We want to learn from Canada and other restricted markets — how do you build acquisition momentum when most activation levers are limited?"
🇷🇴Romania
Region 3 — Europe
MC decision-tree execution and lead pipeline optimisation
MC guided trial decision trees already in use — right product for the right consumer
Call centre well-optimised for quality conversion and retention post-budget cuts
Scaling "bento box" product selection model to reduce cognitive load at POS
Early read on Leads Coin & MGM Coin programs — measuring impact and refining
Workshop priority
"We want to break acquisition into phases and build segment-specific recipes — Experience Explorers need a different journey than Tobacco Progressives."
🇬🇷Greece
Region 3 — Europe
Strengthening MC acquisition across brand engagement channels
Strong brand engagement foundation with experienced consumer-facing teams
Active LAMP and events programme building desirability and brand momentum
Translating upper-funnel brand engagement into measurable downstream acquisition
Building a clearer MC navigation logic for frontline teams across channels
Workshop priority
"We want to strengthen the link between our brand events and actual lead generation — and learn how other markets have made that conversion happen reliably."
🇨🇦Canada
Region 4 — Americas
Precision acquisition in one of the world's most regulated markets
XM / experiential becoming a strong trial driver — especially for P4 at festivals
Manned retail model evolving — neighbourhood retail emerging as a trusted brand channel
Addressing high Sales Expert churn to build consistency in consumer experience
Developing two-way SMS and expanding CRM personalisation beyond channel-based journeys
Workshop priority
"Canada operates in a world where cross-sell, bundles, and most incentives are off the table. We bring real-world constraints — and we want peer learnings that respect them."
🇲🇽Mexico
Region 4 — Americas
Scaling multi-category acquisition across a high-complexity market
Strong portfolio breadth — P1, P4 and P5 all active with dedicated consumer journeys
Consumer engagement marketing model generating strong campaign reach
Building sharper MC navigation logic for frontline across a large, diverse geography
Connecting brand activation to downstream lead data more systematically
Workshop priority
"We want to bring our LAMPs & Events experience to the table and learn how other markets convert engagement into trackable, actionable leads."
